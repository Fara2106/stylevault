How the Stability AI Materials were changed
-------------------------------------------
- Mobile-VTON (Wan et al., 2026) trained its try-on model on top of these
  components; the VAE decoder is Mobile-VTON's own lightweight decoder.
- StyleVault converted the components to Core ML (.mlpackage), compressed the
  weights with 6-bit k-means palettization and packaged them as Apple Archive
  files. No further training was done.
